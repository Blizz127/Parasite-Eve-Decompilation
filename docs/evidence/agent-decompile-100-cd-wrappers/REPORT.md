## Discovery and triage

Instruction-shape clustering over `asm/disc1/*.s` grouped the span's wrappers.
`tools/analysis/try_leaf.py` was extended to zero the object's relocation words
(`R_MIPS_HI16/LO16` and `26`) in **both** the candidate and retail image, so a
call-bearing leaf can be diffed before the real link. All 41 report
`WORDS MATCH`.

Two triage catches that would otherwise have failed the full build:

- The conditional members must be a `switch`, not an `if/else if` chain
  (`bne`/register allocation differs).
- The case values for `func_800867E4`/`func_8008682C` are ordered
  1→0x9B/0x9A, 2→0x9D/0x9C, else→0x99/0x98 (the default lands via the `j`
  delay slot), not the reverse.
